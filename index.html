HOW IT WORKS

DRIVEThe car accelerates on its own. Hold ◀ ▶ to steer and BRAKE to slow down or reverse. FIREMachine gun. Ammo is limited each race. BASHClub whoever is right next to you. ITEMSmash the yellow crates for missiles, mines, oil, turbo, shields and wrenches. POWEREvery racer gets a random super power each race, including the pink Reverse, which flips the whole race direction and reshuffles the order — yours and the rivals'. Fire it when the ring is full. GARAGE & GRIDYou start with some cash. Spend it on repairs and upgrades in the garage before each race, then check the grid screen to see what every rival bought and who's dangerous this time. DIFFICULTYEasy is wide and forgiving. Hard has tight, twisty tracks and a much higher crash rate — try Medium first. RUNGet wrecked or finish last and the run is over. Earn cash for places, wrecks and roadkill. Closing the game mid-run is safe — it picks back up where you left off.

Keyboard: arrows or WASD, Space fire, X item, Z power, C bash, P pause.
